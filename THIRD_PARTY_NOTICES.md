## IP, PLEASE

本项目是 [IP, PLEASE](https://github.com/ip-please/ip-please.github.io) 的非官方改编与扩展，原作者 **ctnyanya**。原作提供像素窗口审查题材、玩法与视觉参考。本仓库在保留上游谱系及 MIT 声明的基础上，采用 TypeScript、Canvas 和 DOM 重实现，并扩展为 31 日流程、模型家庭、来函、救援和 13 个结局。它不是原作者的官方续作，也不把上游成果归作本项目原创。

This is an unofficial adaptation and expansion of [IP, PLEASE](https://github.com/ip-please/ip-please.github.io), originally by **ctnyanya**. The upstream game supplies the inspection-game premise and gameplay/visual references. This repository preserves upstream history and its MIT notice, uses a TypeScript, Canvas and DOM reimplementation, and adds a 31-day campaign, model family, correspondence, rescue sequences and 13 endings. It is not an official sequel and does not claim authorship of upstream work.
