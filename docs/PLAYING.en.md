# Playing IP Please

## The window

Choose Standard or Relaxed, read the newspaper, enter work and read the directive. D1 checks current IP only. D2 adds permits, D3 passports, D4 binding, D5 concrete connection concerns, and D6 commercial authorization. Later days expand checks through actual papers and published rules.

Click the cover to open the book. Papers are separate objects, move freely and overlap; moving the book never carries attachments. Clicking a paper raises it without opening a modal. Read document opens the top paper's full text and pauses reception. Click the physical directive to read the rules.

Click a field once to compare its established counterpart in submitted evidence. Lines and endpoints mark the comparison; they do not decide for you. I toggles hints; Escape hides them. Ask for papers when needed: undelivered supplements do not appear automatically.

Drag a stamp by its handle. Only an exposed area on the right inner page accepts it. Dropping on another paper or outside the book does nothing. Restamp before returning the book to change your decision; final wages and errors follow the final stamp.

Return the book and every delivered attachment separately through any part of the left applicant window, in any order. The next applicant is available only after all are returned. Tabs raise covered papers; the full-text reader also offers individual return controls.

Dialogue advances one line per click even when muted. Earlier lines remain in the scrollable transcript. Approval thanks stay until the next applicant.

## Clock and economy

Both modes allow 90 seconds of accumulated active reception. Time pauses before calling and after every paper is returned. A stamped book or attachment awaiting return still counts as active reception. Settings, reading, forced instructions and backgrounding pause time.

At zero, reception closes immediately. Completed decisions retain wages and fines even if papers remain. Undecided cases earn nothing and are not errors. Finishing every case closes early. Error notices arrive together at closing.

Clearly wrongful approvals forfeit piece rate and incur 10, then 15 on repeats in Standard; 5, then 10 in Relaxed. Wrong rejections receive one warning in Standard or two in Relaxed, then fines of 10 or 5 respectively. Later rules never retrospectively punish earlier compliant decisions.

## Home

Salary and rent settle before supplies. Rent requires at least 20 at deduction; exactly 20 is enough. Failure ends the run. Preview each member before purchasing electricity, tokens or repairs. Packages cost the same regardless of survivors.

Missing resources build pressure and damage over successive nights. Tolerance differs by member. Repairs clear existing damage before calculating tonight's shortages. Destroyed cores cannot be repaired or revived; destruction of the player's core ends the run. Repairs cost 28 for the player, 24 for either parent, 20 for the wife and 18 for the son.

## Later work

D8 introduces before-shift letters, contracts, originals and receipts. Reading, retaining and submitting papers are separate recorded actions. Ordinary paperwork determines consequences; there is no route-selection menu.

D12 introduces review and bans. Review does not ban. A ban requires published grounds and matching enforcement papers. Ordinary rejection uses the rejection stamp. Eligible appeals can reinstate an account from D28.

A surviving dark-green guard can offer a private share of 10 per actual new ban. This is separate from wages and fines. Death ends the agreement; no successor inherits it.

D16 has free training. D18, D23 and D27 have playable rescue. Open the cabinet, take the pulse gun and fire only at marked attackers damaging the gate. Three charges per wave; misses consume one. Some incidents also need abnormal-channel isolation. Standard gives 15 seconds per wave; Relaxed aims step by step. Rescue pauses reception; settings and backgrounding pause rescue. Reloads preserve hits, charges and casualties. D27 has two waves.

Audits use recorded conduct and served deadlines. Expired removal procedures take priority at closing. Previously submitted departure plans execute after rent succeeds. Endings add no activation, transfer or per-person fees. D31 follows already registered handovers, with line-by-line epilogues and up to three echoes based on actual family and guard states.

## Progress and language

There is one automatic run per browser and origin: no manual slots, day selection, rollback or save import/export. Facts are acknowledged after storage succeeds. Reloads resume the case, return, rescue or settlement without duplicating transactions.

Only one tab writes. Takeover loads the latest record and stops the old tab. Failed writes pause play and offer retry. Corrupt or unsupported records are preserved. Restarting in Settings replaces the run after ordinary confirmation. Clearing site data removes it.

Language is stored separately under `ip-please:language`; saved facts keep their existing schema and keys. Switching Chinese / English changes only display text. Cases, cash, timers, records and decisions continue. The language control neither reveals endings nor resets progress.
